### Synthesis
- **Four families, one vocabulary.** (i) charged / ion-sieving layers: sulfonate (Si 2025, Sun 2023, Chen 2024), carboxylate (Gou 2025), Nafion (Wu 2021), negatively charged oxides (Tan 2025, Wang 2024 SiOₓ); (ii) inorganic fillers: MOFs, zeolites, minerals, BaTiO₃, HfO₂, ZrO₂, CeF₃, kaolin; (iii) carbon: insulating/functional (GO, g-C₃N₄, NDs) vs conductive (rGO, Ketjen black, vertical graphene) — conductive layers move deposition onto the separator (Lin 2023), and the functional side always faces Zn, no paper compares orientations; (iv) structural (BC blend, PVDF). Reviews (Gu 2025, Li 2024, Chen 2026) and almost every paper explain the effect with the same four mechanisms: E-field homogenisation, ion sieving/SO₄²⁻ repulsion, desolvation, Zn(002) texture — usually asserted with DFT, rarely quantified; a surface charge (ζ) is measured in only 3 papers (Tan, Zhang 2023, Cha).
- **The GF baseline is not reproducible.** t₊(GF) 0.13–0.43 across 8 papers (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI 0.13, Huang 0.19, Liu 0.22, Zhao 0.27†, Tan/Kim 0.35, Luo 0.38 — all BV; Cha 0.43 by liquid-junction potential); σ(GF) 3.8–66.6 mS cm⁻¹ (~17×); GF control life ~60–80 h @1/1 (Luo, Liu) but 110–300 h @5/1 (Zhang 2024, Yuan, Cha). Only Kim and Hansen name the GF grade. → "×-fold" improvements compare against different baselines. The group's GF t₊ of 0.51 lies above all of them and above bulk 2 M ZnSO₄ (0.197, Purser & Stokes 1951); even the bulk reference is disputed (Cha cites ≈ 0.40).
- **No consistent σ–t₊ trade-off.** σ falls while t₊ rises in 3 papers (Huang, Liu, Kim's intermediate step) but rises with t₊ in 5 (Luo 2024, Kim final, Zhang 2023, Cha, Zhao†). A Donnan-type fixed-charge picture predicts lower σ when anions are excluded → the reported pairs are more likely dominated by wetting, uptake and measurement artefacts than by charge selectivity (inference; fits our models 03/04). Huang gets a ~9× life gain with t₊·σ unchanged (calc) → structural, not transport.
- **Test conditions flatter the results.** Headline Zn//Zn runs use 0.5–5 mA cm⁻² and ≤ 1 mAh cm⁻²; Zn thickness missing in 4 of 9 full-text papers; known headline DOD 0.85–3.4 % (calc). Only 2 papers state a high DOD (Yang 2022 42.7 %, Zhang 2022 56 % → 220 h). Soft shorts can mimic stable cycling (Li 2022 Joule) and symmetric-cell gains don't transfer to full cells (Guo 2025). The group's 3738 h is at 1.7 % DOD — typical, not exceptional.
- **GF fails structurally.** Zn grows through the pores to a short (Hansen: 79/80 cells); smaller pores and thicker GF extend life but raise scatter; compression not varied. Fibre loosening (Kim, Liu) and by-product crusts (Huang, Kim). → pore size, thickness and compression are first-order model parameters (2D step).
- **Simulations are illustrative, and NDs on GF are not new.** 4 papers with continuum FEM (Song, Tan, Yuan: COMSOL; Zhang 2023): all 2D, semicircular Zn bumps, coating as a homogeneous layer, BCs not in the main text, no validation; concentration scales 0–10 / 0–4 mol m⁻³ vs 2000 mol m⁻³ at 2 M. DFT is never linked to the continuum model. A direct ND@GF precedent exists (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI: t₊ 0.13 → 0.45, 110 → 1800 h @5/1, no simulation) → the gap is the *mechanistic, quantitative* model of an ND-modified separator, not ND@GF itself (§2.5).
- **Who:** 33 CN / 7 INT / 6 unknown. The only systematic GF-baseline study (Hansen) and the only non-BV t₊ (Cha) are INT.
