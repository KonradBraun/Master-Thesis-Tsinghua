### Synthesis
- **Four families, one vocabulary.** (i) charged / ion-sieving layers: sulfonate (Si 2025, Sun 2023, Chen 2024), carboxylate (Gou 2025), Nafion (Wu 2021), negatively charged oxides (Tan 2025, Wang 2024 SiOₓ); (ii) inorganic fillers: MOFs, zeolites, minerals, BaTiO₃, HfO₂, ZrO₂, CeF₃, kaolin; (iii) carbon: insulating/functional (GO, g-C₃N₄, NDs) vs conductive (rGO, Ketjen black, vertical graphene) — conductive layers move deposition onto the separator (Lin 2023; Lee 2024 shows Zn plating on its conductive layers), and the functional side always faces Zn, no paper compares orientations; (iv) structural (BC blend, PVDF). Reviews (Gu 2025, Li 2024, Chen 2026) and almost every paper explain the effect with the same four mechanisms: E-field homogenisation, ion sieving/SO₄²⁻ repulsion, desolvation, Zn(002) texture — usually asserted with DFT, rarely quantified; a surface charge (ζ) is measured in only 3 papers (Tan, Zhang 2023, Cha).
- **The GF baseline is not reproducible.** t₊(GF) 0.13–0.55 across 14 papers (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI 0.13, Huang 0.19, Liu 0.22, Liang 0.24, Zhao 2024 0.27, Zhao 2025 0.28, Yuan 0.29, Sun 0.33, Tan/Kim 0.35, Luo 0.38, Shao 0.54, Zhang 2022 0.55 — BV or DC polarisation; Cha 0.43 by liquid-junction potential); σ(GF) 3.0–66.6 mS cm⁻¹ (~22×, calc); GF control life ~60–80 h @1/1 (Luo, Liu) but 110–300 h @5/1 (Zhang 2024, Yuan, Cha). Only Kim, Hansen, Gu, Sun (GF/A) and Yuan (GF/C, in the SI) name the GF grade. → "×-fold" improvements compare against different baselines. The group's GF t₊ of 0.51 lies above all but Shao (0.54) and Zhang 2022 (0.55) and above bulk 2 M ZnSO₄ (0.197, Purser & Stokes 1951); even the bulk reference is disputed (Cha cites ≈ 0.40).
- **No consistent σ–t₊ trade-off.** σ falls while t₊ rises in 4 papers (Huang, Liu, Kim's intermediate step, Sun 2023: 25.0 → 17.2 mS cm⁻¹ with 0.33 → 0.58) but rises with t₊ in 8 (Luo 2024, Kim final, Zhang 2023, Cha, Shao, Zhang 2022, Yuan, Zhao 2024: 10.1 → 13.7 mS cm⁻¹ with 0.27 → 0.37). A Donnan-type fixed-charge picture predicts lower σ when anions are excluded → the reported pairs are more likely dominated by wetting, uptake and measurement artefacts than by charge selectivity (inference; fits our models 03/04). Huang gets a ~9× life gain with t₊·σ unchanged (calc) → structural, not transport.
- **Test conditions flatter the results.** Headline Zn//Zn runs use 0.5–5 mA cm⁻² and ≤ 1 mAh cm⁻²; Zn thickness missing in 4 of 9 full-text papers; known headline DOD 0.85–3.4 % (calc). Only 3 papers state a high DOD (Yang 2022 42.7 %, Shao 2025 42.70 % @5/5 → 920 h vs ~26 h GF (calc), Zhang 2022 56 % → 220 h at 28.3 mA cm⁻²/28.3 mAh cm⁻² with ≈ 85 µm Zn — the only one that states the Zn thickness); Yang 2022 gives neither j/Q nor Zn thickness in its main text, so reading its 42.7 % as 20 µm Zn at 5 mAh cm⁻² (like Shao) stays an inference. Zhang 2022 proposes DOD ≥ 31 % as benchmark; with 100 µm Zn most studies use < 1 % of the Zn (Li 2022 Joule, §2.1). Soft shorts can mimic stable cycling — falling overpotential and R_ct, no voltage drop; Li 2022 Joule proposes R_ct checks, T-dependent Eₐ and Zn//Cu cells as tests, none of which the separator papers here report (inference from the full texts read) and symmetric-cell gains don't transfer to full cells (Guo 2025). The group's GF and ND@GF cells were tested at 1.7 % DOD under identical conditions (proposal text: no comment on the group's DOD — student, 12 Nov).
- **GF fails structurally.** Zn grows through the pores to a short (Hansen: 79/80 cells); smaller pores and thicker GF extend life but raise scatter (Hansen; confirmed by Gu 2025: 2×GF/A 300 vs 100 h @3/3, denser GF/F beats GF/A); compression not varied. Fibre loosening (Kim, Liu) and by-product crusts (Huang, Kim). → pore size, thickness and compression are first-order model parameters (2D step).
- **Simulations are illustrative, and NDs on GF are not new.** 8 papers with continuum FEM (Song, Tan, Yuan, Shao, Li 2020, Gou 2025, Su 2022, Zhang 2023): mostly 2D (Li 2020, Gou 2025 simplified 3D), semicircular Zn bumps, coating as a homogeneous layer, BCs not in the main text, no validation; concentration scales 0–10 / 0–4 mol m⁻³ vs 2000 mol m⁻³ at 2 M. Across 14 papers (§2.4, B1) 5 name the interface, 1 states BCs, 3 give parameter values (2 implausible), none has a mesh study or validation; Yuan's SI draws the 250 µm separator 2–3 µm thick. Zhang 2026 Adv. Sci. (fibres as circles/ellipses) and Wang 2024 AFM (100 nm ND particles) are the only Zn papers found that resolve particles or fibres — the same idealisation as the group's "hollow circles". DFT is never linked to the continuum model. A direct ND@GF precedent exists (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI: t₊ 0.13 → 0.45, 110 → 1800 h @5/1, no simulation) → the gap is the *mechanistic, quantitative* model of an ND-modified separator, not ND@GF itself (§2.5).
- **Who:** 34 CN / 7 INT / 5 unknown. The only systematic GF-baseline study (Hansen) and the only non-BV t₊ (Cha) are INT.

### Synthesis
- **The sign of the ND surface charge is set by termination and any sp² shell, not by "diamond".** O-terminated/oxidised ND is negative (−35 to −55 mV, Ginés; −45 mV, Girard; −41 mV, Ding; −50 to −100 mV for ozone-purified ND, pH 2–12, Mochalin — all in water); positive ζ needs H-termination plus sp² (Ginés) or specific cleaning (+60 mV, Paci); as-received DND can be ~0 (Molavi). Literature can't give the sign for the group's coal-derived ND (sp² present per Zhang 2026 XPS) → the group's ζ of ND@GF (~−38.8 mV, `../notes/parameters.md`) has no stated medium/pH and is almost certainly not in 2 M ZnSO₄ (inference).
- **In 2 M ZnSO₄ the double layer is collapsed and the charge may invert.** Debye length ~0.1 nm (estimate, ideal); ND dispersions aggregate from 10⁻³–10⁻² M (Bradac via Shalumon; Bradac itself: once ζ is within ~±35 mV, tested to 1 M). Ding 2026 is the only Zn paper with ζ in electrolyte: ~−10 mV at 0.5 m, ~0 at ~1.5 m, positive above — Zn²⁺ adsorption inverts the charge. → a fixed *negative* charge on NDs at 2 M is not supported; specific Zn²⁺ adsorption (Kulakova: cation exchange; Volkov: Zn²⁺ uptake > 100 µmol g⁻¹, chemisorption sites saturated by 10–15 mmol L⁻¹ — far below 2 M, so the ND surface should be fully Zn²⁺-loaded in the cell, inference) is the more defensible surface model. Consistent with models 03/04 (fixed charge negligible).
- **Claims rest on DFT, not measurement.** Zincophilicity, low barriers and "field homogenisation by carboxyl groups" come from DFT/MD or are asserted (Liu 2024, Wang 2024, Zhu 2026, Zhang 2024 ACS AMI); hardness only in Chen 2024 (Zn) and Li papers (Cheng 2017, Zhou 2021). Measured: t₊, Eₐ, nucleation overpotential, cycle life — ζ only in Ding 2026.
- **Same electrolyte, different t₊ baselines:** 0.13 (Zhang 2024), 0.20 (Zhu 2026), 0.52 (Ding 2026), plus the group's 0.51 — all BV; "t₊ gains" from ND are method-dependent (cf. §2.2, §2.4).
- **Surface-site densities for a boundary condition** (order of magnitude): acid groups 0.55–1.47 mmol g⁻¹ (Kulakova), ~3.3 sites nm⁻² (Paci, calc), OH ~1 nm⁻² (Girard), Zn²⁺ uptake > 100 µmol g⁻¹ (Volkov via Shalumon).
- **Gap:** no continuum model resolves an ND layer or ND-coated fibres; the nearest analogue (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI) has no simulation; Ding 2026 uses COMSOL only at cell level. Zn–ND work is CN-dominated (one Jilin superhard-materials group accounts for 2 of the ND papers here plus Zhang 2024 Commun. Chem., not added); the ND surface-chemistry basics are all INT.

### Synthesis
- **Dendrite onset theory is analytical and 1D, and none of it is for 2 M ZnSO₄.** Sand-type models with kinetic (Yang 2025), SEI (Ma JES) or HER-bubble (Ma JPS, alkaline) corrections; short-circuit times are far longer than onset times and propagation through the separator is not modelled. Validated Zn sulfate continuum models are rare (Shan 2023 flow cell; Telmasre 2025, ohmic-only, validated visually) → a 2D tertiary model of a GF/ND@GF cell at the measured operating point would be new.
- **Charged-pore physics exists but isn't used by Zn separator papers.** Surface conduction in charged nanopores (Han 2014) and immobilised anions (Tikekar 2016) can stabilise deposition, but only above the limiting current in dilute electrolyte (Han: 10 mM, 300–400 nm pores; gone at 1 M) or with a fixed-charge fraction ~0.1 of the salt (Tikekar); Donnan partitioning is weak for bivalent ions and needs high charge per pore volume (Vezzani 2002, Galama 2013). With Debye ~0.1 nm at 2 M and ND charge inversion (§2.3), a fixed-charge ND layer should have small effects — what models 03/04 found.
- **Microstructure matters beyond ε and τ.** Pore connectivity changes concentration gradients at equal porosity/tortuosity (Lagadec 2018: PE vs PP, both ~40 %; a 2D structure reaches only intermediate connectivity → a 2D pore model of GF will understate 3D bypass paths, inference); separator pore size sets dendrite regimes in Li PF models (Jana 2015: fibres as inert circles, 4 regimes, analytical critical current; Li 2022 CCL) — the fibre-as-circle geometry is the same idealisation as the group's "hollow circles" for NDs. No image-based or pore-resolved model of a GF separator for Zn exists (in this search) → the 2D pore-structure step targets a real gap.
- **Phase-field in Zn: borrowed parameters, qualitative validation, no separator.** γ = 0.5 J m⁻² and ω = 6 are reused from Cogswell; anisotropy strength (0.01–0.4), i₀ (28 A m⁻² to unsourced mA cm⁻²) and even D (10⁻¹¹ vs 3.7×10⁻¹⁰ m² s⁻¹) vary between papers; only Cogswell validates quantitatively (zincate). The closest analogue to an ND layer is Pant 2023 (coating as a region with its own anisotropic D). → phase field is at most an optional qualitative add-on here (own assessment).
- **Atomistic results don't reach the continuum.** DFT/MD papers report adsorption and desolvation energies (Jin 2026; §2.2/§2.3 papers) but none passes them to i₀ or D — consistent with the decision against DFT.
- **BV t₊ is not a calibration target.** BV is exact only at infinite dilution (Shao 2023); multivalent salts: i_ss/i₀ = t₊ only when dilute, otherwise a parameter Ne from all transport coefficients (Balsara 2015) and a common reference frame (Gao 2022); Zn interfaces drift during the test (Yang 2024) and sampling times bias the result (Lin 2024); BV for near-identical Zn electrolytes spans 0.2–0.7 (Gomez Vazquez 2025). → use Hittorf/EMF electrolyte data (Purser & Stokes 1951, Dye 1960) as input and simulate the BV protocol itself (model 04), comparing only trends.
- **Field maps in Zn papers are unverified illustrations** (B1 synthesis above: no mesh study or quantitative validation in 14 papers; tip maxima are mesh-dependent singularities, Shah 2020; tip effect overstated in 2D, Jang 2021) → reporting BCs, parameters, mesh study and sensitivity is itself a contribution.

### Synthesis
- **The failure modes form one self-reinforcing chain set by the interfacial pH.** Water released during desolvation feeds HER (Xu 2023; Yu 2024 JACS) → local OH⁻ rises → insulating ZHS precipitates in ZnSO₄ (Dong 2022; Cui 2025) → uneven current, Zn grows on protrusions and over the by-products (Cui 2025) → dendrites and encapsulated "dead Zn" lower CE (Dong 2022; Xu 2023); adsorbed OH⁻ also nucleates dendrites at atomic scale (Zhang 2024). The spatial pH field is not measured (Dong) → a continuum target (pH/OH⁻ + precipitation; corrosion option of the thesis).
- **What a separator can and cannot fix.** It acts on transport: flux homogenisation and pore-scale current focusing (Dong names GF thickness and large pores). It cannot remove HER thermodynamics (Zn²⁺/Zn −0.762 V vs SHE vs HER 0 V at pH 0; 2 M ZnSO₄ pH 3.3, Cui), corrosion at rest, or cathode-side H⁺/hydroxide chemistry (Li 2022 Joule). → claims of HER/corrosion suppression by a separator need an indirect mechanism (solvation, pH buffering) — inference.
- **Zn ≈ Li metal, with water.** Shared: dendrites, volume change, need for > 99.9 % CE (Dong). Zn-specific: HER/corrosion, by-products instead of an SEI, higher volumetric capacity (5854 mAh cm⁻³, Dong). Li values still need a source (Kim 2024, needs full text).
- **Practical test criteria.** CE should be measured at counter-electrode DOD < 20 % (Dong); with thin Zn the capacity curve shows an inflection once Zn is used up, and the efficiency before it (88 % per cycle in Wu 2024's example) is far below typical reported CE (Wu 2024); lab cells run at very low Zn utilisation (< 1 % with 100 µm Zn, Li 2022 Joule) and E/C ~1000 g Ah⁻¹ (Xu); in situ microscopy cells differ from coin cells (Yu 2023) — consistent with §2.2 (headline DOD 0.85–3.4 %).

### Synthesis (argument chain)
