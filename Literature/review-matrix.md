### Synthesis (so far — 10 papers)
- **The GF baseline is not reproducible across papers.** t₊(GF) 0.19–0.38 (BV, 10–20 mV; Huang, Liu, Tan, Kim, Luo); σ(GF) 3.82–66.61 mS cm⁻¹ (~17×; Luo, Song, Huang, Kim, Liu); GF control life @1/1 ~60–80 h (Luo, Liu). Only Kim and Hansen name the GF grade. "×-fold" improvements are therefore not comparable between papers. The group's GF t₊ of 0.51 lies above all of these and above bulk 2 M ZnSO₄ (0.197, Purser & Stokes 1951).
- **σ and t₊ don't move together.** σ falls while t₊ rises in 3 of 7 papers with both (Huang, Liu, Kim's intermediate step); Akram's literature map makes the same point. Huang gets a ~9× life gain with t₊·σ unchanged (calc) → mechanism there is structural (thin, uniform pores), not transport.
- **Test conditions flatter the results.** Headline Zn//Zn runs use 0.5–2 mA cm⁻² and 0.25–1 mAh cm⁻²; Zn thickness is missing in 4 of 9 Zn papers; where known, headline DOD is 0.85–3.4 % (calc). At ~17 % DOD, lifetimes drop to ≤ 400 h (Huang, Liu, Zhang).
- **GF fails structurally.** Hansen: Zn grows through the pores to a short (79/80 cells); smaller pores and thicker GF extend life but increase cell-to-cell scatter; compression not varied. Others report fibre loosening/delamination (Kim, Liu) and by-product crusts (Huang, Kim). → pore size, thickness and compression are first-order model parameters.
- **Simulations in separator papers are illustrative.** 3 of 10 have continuum FEM (Song, Tan: COMSOL; Zhang: software n/r): all 2D with semicircular Zn bumps, coating as a homogeneous layer, BCs/parameters not in the main text, no validation; concentration legends 0–10 / 0–4 mol m⁻³ do not match 2 M (2000 mol m⁻³). DFT (Luo, Liu, Song) is not linked to the continuum model. Same pattern as the group's model (NDs as holes) → gap for §2.5.
- **Who:** 7 CN / 3 INT; the only systematic GF-baseline study (Hansen) is INT, the modification papers are mostly CN.
