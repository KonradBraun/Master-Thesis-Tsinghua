### Synthesis
- **Four families, one vocabulary.** (i) charged / ion-sieving layers: sulfonate (Si 2025, Sun 2023, Chen 2024), carboxylate (Gou 2025), Nafion (Wu 2021), negatively charged oxides (Tan 2025, Wang 2024 SiOₓ); (ii) inorganic fillers: MOFs, zeolites, minerals, BaTiO₃, HfO₂, ZrO₂, CeF₃, kaolin; (iii) carbon: insulating/functional (GO, g-C₃N₄, NDs) vs conductive (rGO, Ketjen black, vertical graphene) — conductive layers can host Zn deposition themselves (Lee 2024 shows Zn plating on its conductive layers; Li 2020 calls its VG layer a "3D continuation of Zn anode"; corrected 19 Nov: Lin 2023 removed — its abstract does not say this), and where the orientation is stated the functional side faces Zn (Li 2020, Zhang 2022, Lee 2024; Su's FEM film sits under the separator; not stated in Liu 2024 ACS Nano or Sun 2023); none of the papers read compares orientations; (iv) structural (BC blend, PVDF). Reviews (Gu 2025, Li 2024, Chen 2026) and almost every paper explain the effect with the same four mechanisms: E-field homogenisation, ion sieving/SO₄²⁻ repulsion, desolvation, Zn(002) texture — usually asserted with DFT, rarely quantified. ζ is reported in at least 5 papers (corrected 14 Nov; was "only 3"): Tan −33.8 mV (of the Ti₀.₈₇O₂ nanosheets, SI Fig. S1), Zhang 2023 anionic −23 / cationic +47 mV (nanocellulose-fibre dispersions, Fig. 2d), Cha (ZrO₂ felt, −34.5 mV in water, −5.2 mV in 50 mM ZnSO₄), Shao GF +4.89 → GF@CAU-17 −12.8 mV (Fig. S2), Zhang 2022 Janus −16.7 (unit printed "eV"). Both signs are credited with a higher t₊ (Shao: negative charge restricts SO₄²⁻; Zhang 2023: cationic groups inhibit SO₄²⁻ migration, "electrostatic shielding" — corrected 19 Nov, was "bind SO₄²⁻"; cationic t₊ 0.73 > anionic 0.57). How the 13 table studies with a t₊ pair use it (checked 14 Nov; 8 re-read 19 Nov): all report a higher t₊; 6 link it to smaller concentration gradients/polarisation (Sun, Zhao 2024, Yuan, Zhao 2025, Luo, Zhang 2024 ACS AMI), 4 to more uniform deposition or stability (Liu, Zhang 2022, Huang, Kim — Liu's t₊ paragraph itself names only ion sieving); Tan, Shao, Liang only report it as better transport. Pairs found later: Su 2022 (better transport), Yang 2022 (SO₄²⁻ repulsion, fewer side reactions), Gou 2025 (0.49 → 0.68).
- **The GF baseline is not reproducible.** t₊(GF) 0.13–0.55 across 17 papers (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI 0.13, Huang 0.19, Liu 0.22, Liang 0.24, Su 2022 0.24, Yang 2022 0.26, Zhao 2024 0.27, Zhao 2025 0.28, Yuan 0.29, Sun 0.33, Tan/Kim 0.35, Luo 0.38, Gou 0.49, Shao 0.54, Zhang 2022 0.55 (3 m ZnSO₄) — BV-type or DC polarisation, method not always named; Cha 0.43 by liquid-junction potential; Yang and Gou added 19 Nov); σ(GF) 3.0–66.6 mS cm⁻¹ (~22×, calc); GF control life @1/1 ~30–430 h (Li 2020 30, Luo ~60, Gou 80, Liu ~80, Sun < 90, Yang 2022 ~200, Zhao 2025 ~230, Lee 2024 430 h) and 110–350 h @5/1 (Zhang 2024 110, Yuan 185, Cha 300, Tan < 350) (corrected 19 Nov: was "~60–80 h @1/1 (Luo, Liu) but 110–300 h @5/1", a selective pair). Only Kim, Hansen, Gu, Sun (GF/A) and Yuan (GF/C, in the SI) name the GF grade. → "×-fold" improvements compare against different baselines. The group's GF t₊ of 0.51 lies above all but Shao (0.54) and Zhang 2022 (0.55) and above bulk 2 M ZnSO₄ (0.197, Purser & Stokes 1951); even the bulk reference is disputed (Cha cites ≈ 0.40).
- **No consistent σ–t₊ trade-off.** σ falls while t₊ rises in 4 papers (Huang, Liu, Kim's intermediate step, Sun 2023: 25.0 → 17.2 mS cm⁻¹ with 0.33 → 0.58) but rises with t₊ in 9 (Luo 2024, Kim final, Zhang 2023, Cha, Shao, Zhang 2022, Yuan, Zhao 2024: 10.1 → 13.7 mS cm⁻¹ with 0.27 → 0.37; Su 2022: 3.0 → 14.4 with 0.24 → 0.35, added 19 Nov). A Donnan-type fixed-charge picture predicts lower σ when anions are excluded → the reported pairs are more likely dominated by wetting, uptake and measurement artefacts than by charge selectivity (inference; fits our models 03/04). Huang gets a ~9× life gain with t₊·σ unchanged (calc) → structural, not transport.
- **Test conditions flatter the results.** Headline Zn//Zn runs mostly use 1–5 mA cm⁻² at 1 mAh cm⁻² (exceptions: Zhang 2022 10/10, Zhang 2023 1/4, Yuan and Liang also 10/1); Zn thickness stated in 9 of 21 table papers (Liu, Huang, Kim, Zhang 2023, Sun, Yang 2022, Zhang 2022; Gou and Yuan only in the SI; corrected 19 Nov, was "missing in 4 of 9"); known headline DOD 0.85–6.8 % (calc; Zhang 2023 6.8 %). Only 3 table papers state a high DOD: Yang 2022 42.7 % at 20 mA cm⁻²/25 mAh cm⁻² with ca. 100 µm Zn, > 48 h (Fig. S9); Shao 2025 42.70 % @5/5 → 920 h vs ~26 h GF (calc; Zn thickness only via SI Eq. S4); Zhang 2022 56 % → 220 h at 28.3 mA cm⁻²/28.3 mAh cm⁻² with ≈ 85 µm Zn (corrected 19 Nov: Yang 2022 does state j/Q and Zn thickness — its txt was unreadable; Zhang 2022 is not the only one). Zhang 2022 proposes DOD ≥ 31 % as benchmark; with 100 µm Zn most studies use < 1 % of the Zn (Li 2022 Joule, §2.1). Soft shorts can mimic stable cycling — falling overpotential and R_ct, no voltage drop; Li 2022 Joule proposes R_ct checks, T-dependent Eₐ from cycling and Zn//Cu cells to define life. Most separator papers here run Zn//Cu cells, but as CE tests, and their Eₐ comes from T-dependent EIS of fresh cells; none uses these tests to rule out soft shorts in its Zn//Zn life (inference from the full texts read; corrected 19 Nov, was "none of which the separator papers report"). The group's GF and ND@GF cells were tested at 1.7 % DOD under identical conditions (proposal text: no comment on the group's DOD — student, 12 Nov).
- **GF fails structurally.** Failure is a dendrite short (Hansen: 79/80 cells show an overpotential collapse consistent with it; micro-CT shows Zn deposits filling the pores); smaller pores and thicker GF extend life but raise scatter (Hansen; consistent with Gu 2025, one cell each: 2×GF/A ~235 vs GF/A ~90 h @3/3, denser GF/F ~250 h — corrected 19 Nov, was "300 vs 100 h"); compression not varied (5.8 MPa crimping in Hansen). Fibre loosening (Kim, Liu) and by-products on the GF surface (Huang) or the Zn anode (Kim, XRD) (corrected 19 Nov, was "by-product crusts (Huang, Kim)"). → pore size, thickness and compression are first-order model parameters (2D step).
- **Simulations are illustrative, and NDs on GF are not new.** 8 papers with continuum FEM (Song, Tan, Yuan, Shao, Li 2020, Gou 2025, Su 2022, Zhang 2023): mostly 2D (Li 2020, Gou 2025 simplified 3D), semicircular Zn bumps, coating as a homogeneous layer, BCs not in the main text (Gou, Su, Yuan give them in the SI), no validation; concentration scales 0–10 mol m⁻³ (Tan, Fig. 5 rendered; the "0–4" scale not re-located 19 Nov) vs 2000 mol m⁻³ at 2 M. Across 14 papers (§2.4, B1) 6 name the interface, 4 state BCs, 6 give parameter values (2 implausible), none has a mesh study or validation (corrected 19 Nov, was "5 / 1 / 3" — stale since the 13 Nov SI reads; Yuan SI states BCs); Yuan's SI draws the 250 µm separator 2–3 µm thick, Su's and Gou's 3 µm. Zhang 2026 Adv. Sci. (fibres as circles/ellipses), Wang 2024 AFM (100 nm ND particles) and Wang 2026 Nano-Micro Lett. (ZnO blocks traced from SEM) are the only Zn papers found that resolve particles or fibres — the same idealisation as the group's "hollow circles" (corrected 19 Nov: Wang 2026 was missing, cf. B1 counts). DFT is never linked to the continuum model. A direct ND@GF precedent exists (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI: t₊ 0.13 → 0.45, 110 → 1800 h @5/1, no simulation) → the gap is the *mechanistic, quantitative* model of an ND-modified separator, not ND@GF itself (§2.5).
- **Who:** mostly CN. The only systematic GF-baseline study (Hansen) and the only non-BV t₊ (Cha) are INT.

### Synthesis
- **The sign of the ND surface charge is set by termination and any sp² shell, not by "diamond".** O-terminated/oxidised ND is negative (−35 to −55 mV, Ginés; −45 mV, Girard; −41 mV, Ding; −50 to −100 mV for ozone-purified ND, pH 2–12, Mochalin — all in water); positive ζ needs H-termination plus sp² (Ginés) or specific cleaning (+60 mV, Paci); as-received DND can be ~0 (Molavi). Literature can't give the sign for the group's coal-derived ND (sp² present per Zhang 2026 XPS) → the group's ζ of ND@GF (~−38.8 mV, `../notes/parameters.md`) has no stated medium/pH and is almost certainly not in 2 M ZnSO₄ (inference).
- **In 2 M ZnSO₄ the double layer is collapsed and the charge may invert.** Debye length ~0.1 nm (estimate, ideal); ND dispersions aggregate with rising salt (Bradac, NaCl ≤ 1 M: phenol/pyrone DND from ~10⁻³–10⁻² M, carboxylated DND visibly from ~10⁻¹ M; both ζ ~0 and > 1 µm at 1 M). Ding 2026 is the only Zn paper with ζ in electrolyte: ~−10 mV at 0.5 m, ~0 at ~1.5 m, ~+8 mV at 2 M, which the authors attribute to Zn²⁺ accumulating at the surface; size ~30 → ~300 nm in 2 M. → a fixed *negative* charge on NDs at 2 M is not supported; specific Zn²⁺ adsorption (Kulakova: cation exchange; Volkov: Zn²⁺ chemisorption ~16 µmol g⁻¹ levels off below ~10–15 mmol L⁻¹, weaker uptake keeps rising to 200–250 mmol L⁻¹, the highest tested — so the strong sites should be occupied in the cell, inference) is the more defensible surface model. Consistent with models 03/04 (fixed charge negligible).
- **Claims rest on DFT, not measurement.** Zincophilicity, low barriers and "field homogenisation by carboxyl groups" come from DFT/MD or are asserted (Liu 2024, Wang 2024, Zhu 2026, Zhang 2024 ACS AMI); hardness only in Chen 2024 (Zn) and Li papers (Cheng 2017, Zhou 2021). Measured: t₊, Eₐ, nucleation overpotential, cycle life — with opposite trends: Eₐ falls with ND in Zhang 2024 ACS AMI, Wang 2024 and Zhu 2026 but rises in Ding 2026, nucleation overpotential falls in Zhang and Zhu but rises in Ding. ζ in electrolyte only in Ding 2026 (Wang 2024: negative ζ, medium in its SI, not read).
- **Different t₊ baselines in similar electrolytes:** 0.13 (Zhang 2024, 2 M ZnSO₄, BV 10 mV), 0.52 (Ding 2026, 2 M ZnSO₄, 25 mV), 0.20 (Zhu 2026, ZnSO₄/Zn(OTf)₂ mixture, method in SI not read), plus the group's 0.51; "t₊ gains" from ND are method-dependent (cf. §2.2, §2.4).
- **Surface-site densities for a boundary condition** (order of magnitude): acid groups 0.55–1.47 mmol g⁻¹ (Kulakova), ~3.3 sites nm⁻² (Paci, calc), OH ~1 nm⁻² (Girard), Zn²⁺ chemisorption ~16 µmol g⁻¹ (Volkov, Tab. 6, pH 2.5; 100–300 µmol g⁻¹ only incl. physisorption at higher concentrations).
- **Gap:** no continuum model resolves an ND layer or ND-coated fibres; the nearest analogue (Zhang 2024 ACS AMI) has no simulation; Ding 2026 uses COMSOL only at cell level. Zn–ND work is CN-dominated (one Jilin superhard-materials group accounts for 2 of the ND papers here plus Zhang 2024 Commun. Chem., not added); the ND surface-chemistry basics are all INT.

Proposed `use-*` (T4): Liu 2024 `use-core` (closest ND-coating precedent: thickness optimum 5.8 µm, but no ζ and an underived site density — a gap the model can address).

### Synthesis
- **Dendrite onset theory is analytical and 1D, and none of it is for 2 M ZnSO₄.** Sand-type models with kinetic (Yang 2025, checked against 1 M Zn(OTf)₂), SEI (Ma JES, Li) or HER-bubble (Ma JPS, alkaline) corrections; short-circuit times are far longer than onset times (Ma JES, Li coin cells) and propagation through the separator is not modelled. Zn sulfate continuum models compared with experiment are rare, and the comparison is qualitative (Shan 2023 flow cell: current extremes "consistent with" measured Zn thickness; Telmasre 2025, ohmic-only, visual comparison — Telmasre not re-checked 19 Nov, no local text) → a 2D tertiary model of a GF/ND@GF cell at the measured operating point would be new (corrected 19 Nov, was "Validated Zn sulfate continuum models are rare (Shan…)").
- **Charged-pore physics exists but isn't used by Zn separator papers.** Surface conduction in charged nanopores (Han 2014) and immobilised anions (Tikekar 2016) can stabilise deposition, but in Han only clearly above the limiting current in dilute electrolyte (10 mM, 300–400 nm pores; at 1 M no over-limiting current and only a slight deposit difference) or, in Tikekar's theory, with a fixed-charge fraction ~0.1 of the salt; for bivalent ions Donnan alone under-predicts NF rejection (Vezzani 2002), and the fixed charge has to be referred to the pore-water volume (Galama 2013) (corrected 19 Nov: was "gone at 1 M" and "Donnan partitioning is weak for bivalent ions and needs high charge per pore volume" — neither source says that). With Debye ~0.1 nm at 2 M and ND charge inversion (§2.3), a fixed-charge ND layer should have small effects — what models 03/04 found.
- **Microstructure matters beyond ε and τ.** Pore connectivity changes concentration gradients at equal porosity/tortuosity (Lagadec 2018: PE vs PP, both ~40 %; its "2D" artificial structure — pores along 2 directions in a 3D cube — has only intermediate connectivity; that a 2D pore model of GF will understate 3D bypass paths is our inference, not tested there; corrected 19 Nov); separator pore size sets dendrite regimes in Li PF models (Jana 2015: fibres as inert circles, 4 regimes, analytical critical current; Li 2022 CCL) — the fibre-as-circle geometry is the same idealisation as the group's "hollow circles" for NDs. No image-based or pore-resolved model of a GF separator for Zn exists (in this search) → the 2D pore-structure step targets a real gap.
- **Phase-field in Zn: borrowed parameters, qualitative validation, no separator.** γ = 0.5 J m⁻² (Pant, Sun 2026) and six-fold anisotropy (Jing ω = 6) follow Cogswell; anisotropy strength (0.01 Cogswell, 0.1 Sun 2026, 0.3 Jing), i₀ (28 A m⁻² Cogswell/Sun 2026, 15 to > 30 A m⁻² Jing) and even the electrolyte D (10⁻¹¹ Pant vs 3.68×10⁻¹⁰ m² s⁻¹ Cogswell, Jing) vary between papers; only Cogswell validates quantitatively (ZnSO₄: Tafel data, fractal dimension, tip velocity) (corrected 19 Nov: was "(zincate)", "0.01–0.4" and "unsourced mA cm⁻²", not found in the PF texts). - **Atomistic results don't reach the continuum.** DFT/MD papers report adsorption and desolvation energies (Jin 2026; §2.2/§2.3 papers) but none passes them to i₀ or D — consistent with the decision against DFT.
- **BV t₊ is not a calibration target.** BV is exact only at infinite dilution (Shao 2023); multivalent salts: i_ss/i₀ = t₊ only when dilute, otherwise a parameter Ne from all transport coefficients (Balsara 2015) and a common reference frame (Gao 2022); Zn interfaces drift during the test (Yang 2024) and sampling times bias the result (Lin 2024, Li/PEO cells); BV values cited for near-identical Zn electrolytes span 0.2–0.7 (30 vs 31 m ZnCl₂, cited by Gomez Vazquez 2025). ### Synthesis
- **The failure modes form one self-reinforcing chain set by the interfacial pH.** Water released during desolvation feeds HER (Xu 2023; Yu 2024 JACS — abstract only) → local OH⁻ rises → insulating hydroxide-Zn salts (Dong 2022), ZHS by XRD in ZnSO₄ (Cui 2025) → uneven current, Zn grows laterally over the non-conductive by-products (Cui 2025) → dendrites and encapsulated "dead Zn" lower CE (Dong 2022; Xu 2023); first-principles calculations find that adsorbed OH⁻/O²⁻ induce dendrite nucleation (Zhang 2024). Spatially resolving the pH next to the Zn surface is "still challenging" (Dong 2022); Rana 2025 imaged a more basic interfacial layer by fluorescence in 1 M ZnCl₂ (§2.4) → a continuum target (pH/OH⁻ + precipitation; corrosion option of the thesis) (corrected 19 Nov, was "The spatial pH field is not measured (Dong)").
- **What a separator can and cannot fix.** It acts on transport: flux homogenisation and pore-scale current focusing (Dong names GF thickness and large pores). It cannot remove HER thermodynamics (Zn²⁺/Zn −0.762 V vs SHE vs HER 0 V at pH 0; 2 M ZnSO₄ pH 3.3, Cui), corrosion at rest, or cathode-side H⁺/hydroxide chemistry (Li 2022 Joule). → claims of HER/corrosion suppression by a separator need an indirect mechanism (solvation, pH buffering) — inference.
- **Zn ≈ Li metal, with water.** Shared: dendrites, volume change, need for > 99.9 % CE (Dong). Zn-specific: HER/corrosion, by-products instead of an SEI, higher volumetric capacity (5854 mAh cm⁻³, Dong). Li values still need a source (Kim 2024, needs full text).
- **Practical test criteria.** CE should be measured at counter-electrode DOD < 20 % (Dong); with thin Zn the capacity curve of a Zn‖Cu cell shows an inflection once Zn is used up, and the efficiency before it (88 % per cycle in Wu 2024's example) is far below typical reported CE (Wu 2024); full cells with < 0.5 mAh cm⁻² cathodes use < 1 % of 100 µm Zn (Li 2022 Joule), and flooded Swagelok-cell studies reach E/C up to 1000 g Ah⁻¹ (Xu); in situ microscopy cells differ from coin cells (Yu 2023) — consistent with §2.2 (headline DOD 0.85–6.8 %) (corrected 19 Nov: Xu "E/C ~1000" scope; §2.2 range updated).

### Synthesis (argument chain)
- Grid and long-duration storage need very low energy-capacity cost, ≤ US$20 kWh⁻¹ (Sepulveda 2021; Ziegler 2019), plus safety and long life (Dunn 2011). Li-ion faces supply-chain risk mainly for Co (DRC > 50 % of mining; Li limited by production ramp-up, not reserves — Olivetti 2017 p. 234–241) and flammable electrolytes (Dong 2022 p. 1); thermal runaway, vent-gas and fire hazards in stationary BESS lack an agreed suppression strategy ("absence of consensus", Wang 2025 abs; Gateway BESS fire 2024 took two weeks to extinguish, p. 2), driven by a chain of exothermic reactions from SEI decomposition at 80–120 °C upwards (Feng 2018 §4.3). Cost and cycle life per technology (Mongird 2020, Tab. 7, 2018 values, 4 h systems): Li-ion US$271 kWh⁻¹ capital / 469 total, RTE 0.86, 3500 cycles at 80 % DoD; the only Zn system (near-neutral aqueous Zn hybrid, EoS) is about as cheap per kWh (265 / 551) but less efficient (RTE 0.72) and much less mature (TRL 5 vs 8) → all battery capital costs are ≥ 13× the ≤ US$20 kWh⁻¹ long-duration target (calc; different cost bases — Sepulveda's energy-capacity cost vs Mongird's capital cost, inference); AZIB appeal is safety and materials, not yet cost (own assessment).
